HELFEN, HEILEN UND WEHREN!

Infiltrate the Castle

Clear of Piccolo, away from the cook and his ladle, we break the seal.

Messengers — you asked to be trusted. Here is how you earn it. Before this night is over, we move against the Order, but we move half-blind. The Komtur of the castle knows more about us than we would like, and we need to know what he knows.

A proud man walks outside his own walls as if the city were already his. Wear another face — the carnival lends you one for free. Offer him a whiff of a name, a rumour, and let him talk.
