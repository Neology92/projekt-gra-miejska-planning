The Rule for Toruń Piernik

as every honest oven in this town has kept it

Rye flour
three measures — the body of the dough
Wheat flour
one measure — for a smoother crumb
Honey
as much as the dough will take
Pounded spices
cinnamon, ginger, cloves, cardamom, nutmeg, pepper
Candied orange peel
a little, for brightness

Every piernik holds these — but not every jar before you does.
