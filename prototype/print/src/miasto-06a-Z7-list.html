Whatever road brought it into your hands, here it is — a letter the Order never meant any of you to read. A dispatch from the castle, taken on its way to Marienburg, never delivered. And the heading is the same hand, the same proud script, as the list of names you carry. The two belong together.

OLSMLU, OLPSLU BUK DLOYLU!

To the Hochmeister at Marienburg. In haste — and for your eyes alone.

What you asked of me, I have. I will not trust it to any road in plain words — too many hands lie between here and Marienburg. I set it down by our word, as we agreed:

OHUZ CVU IHFZLU PZ QVYKHU AOL JVVR

Read it, then burn it. If this falls into the wrong hands before you, our word still keeps it shut.

— by the hand of the Komtur of Thorn

The Order's own word locks it — but you hold their word too, headed across the list of names. Set the one against the other.
