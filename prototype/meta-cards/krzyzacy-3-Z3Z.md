## You tried to do what the real Order never could.

The Order *knew*. From **November 1453** its informers whispered that the Council was plotting; letters reached Malbork, and someone sat close enough to report the meeting dates. And still nothing was done — barely **three hundred brothers** in all of Prussia, no money, no allies. Knowing is not the same as being able.

Tonight you slipped into the enemy's kitchen and leaned on their man. Real agents tried exactly this in 1454, and failed: the Order's spy network was sharp, its army wasn't.

"Piccolo," by the way, is pure anachronism — an Italian name no 15th-century tavern could wear. In 1454 it's just a kitchen and a ladle. Though the man behind that ladle is worth more than you've been told.
