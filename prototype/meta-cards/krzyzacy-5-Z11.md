## ⚠ You played the side history was about to bury — and you nearly had them.

The Order knew everything and still lost. By **1466** it had signed away Pomerania, Warmia, even Malbork, kneeling as a vassal of the Polish crown. This castle was never rebuilt — torn down stone by stone for 85 marks, the ground turned into a rubbish tip.

Your cipher and the nine o'clock bell were fair play: the Order really did encrypt its letters, the Caesar shift is as old as Rome, and that bell was never the famous *Tuba Dei* — that one wasn't cast until 1500.

And the secret the townsmen's couriers chased all game? Their humble "Jordan the cook" was **Hans von Baysen — Jan Bażyński** — chairman of the Council, a man the Order itself had raised as a diplomat before he turned. Your enemy's hero was your enemy's traitor. You, at least, always knew whose side you were on.
