## That wasn't a raftsman's pipe. It was an African thumb piano.

The instrument in your hands was a **kalimba** — a lamellophone from sub-Saharan Africa, its metal tongues plucked by the thumbs. It didn't reach Europe until the **1800s**. In 1454 Toruń it's flatly impossible, so we dressed it as a raftsman's pipe and hoped the seam wouldn't show.

The tune you played spelled a date: **6 · 2 · 1454** — the morning the townsfolk stood at the castle gate and demanded its surrender.

Even the raftsman's legend — the piper who lured a plague of frogs into the Vistula — is a 19th-century tale, a cousin of the Pied Piper of Hamelin. And that flisak fountain by the Town Hall you might picture? **1914.**

One real thing, wrapped in three inventions. That's most of history, honestly.
