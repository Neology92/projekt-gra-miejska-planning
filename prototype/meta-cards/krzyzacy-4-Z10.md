## The mural you hunted is older than the revolt itself.

The wall paintings in that house on Szeroka Street — dancing courtiers, lovers, wild men riding goats — are genuine, and they date to about **1370**. In 1454 they were already eighty years old: still there, still admired, but not new. You were reading antiques.

The merchant family who owned the house painted knightly, courtly scenes because that's who they wished to be — burghers dreaming themselves into nobility. Half the quarrel of 1454 hides in that wish: merchants who fancied themselves knights, against the real knights of the Order.

The "Crusader bestiary" you matched them against? That one's ours. The beasts are real medieval images — but the *document* never existed. We borrowed the menagerie and invented the catalogue.
