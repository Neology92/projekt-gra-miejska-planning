## That wasn't a cloister psaltery. It was an African thumb piano.

The instrument in your hands was a **kalimba** — a lamellophone from sub-Saharan Africa, plucked with the thumbs, unknown in Europe until the **1800s**. In 1454 it's impossible, so we dressed it as a cloister psaltery and hoped the seam wouldn't show.

The tune you played spelled a date: **6 · 2 · 1454** — the morning the townsfolk demanded the castle's surrender, and the day your Order began to lose Prussia. You played the date of your own defeat.

Somewhere across the city, at that very hour, the *other* side played the *same* tune on the *same* instrument — and to them it sounded like victory. One kalimba, two worlds. Compare notes afterwards.
