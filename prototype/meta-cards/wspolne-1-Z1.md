## Almost nothing you walked past was really there in 1454.

The Copernicus statue where your trail ended was cast in **1853** — and Copernicus himself wasn't born until **1473**, nineteen years *after* the revolt you're living through. No astronomer. No monument. Nothing.

The gingerbread woman in bronze? **2011.** The donkey with the cruel iron ridge along its back? A 2007 copy of a pillory that first appeared in **1629**.

Only one stop was truly there: the patrician house behind the golden star. Its medieval walls stood in 1454 — the star and the proud date **1697** came two centuries later.

We sent you chasing modern statues to find a medieval city. It's still down there. You just have to subtract a few hundred years.
