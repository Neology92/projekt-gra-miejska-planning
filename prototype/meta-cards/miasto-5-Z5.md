## Everything you smelled was real 1454 — except the one sweet thing.

Toruń's gingerbread is genuinely medieval, prized since the 1300s and shipped across the Hanseatic world. What you sorted by smell and weight was an honest 15th-century recipe: **rye** flour (never wheat — wheat was the patricians' white bread), forest **honey** for sweetness, and spices hauled all the way from Italy at a price that showed off exactly how rich this city was.

What was *not* here in 1454: **sugar icing** (cane sugar was an imported luxury), and the heart-shaped *katarzynki* — those are a 19th-century invention with a love story bolted on afterwards.

The paprika and cocoa among your jars? New World — a century or more too early. Little traps for sharp noses.

The guilds guarded recipes like state secrets. Now you know one.
