## Everything you smelled was real 1454 — but the story you were told about it wasn't quite.

The gingerbread was genuine medieval craft: **rye** flour, forest **honey**, and costly spices from Italy. The bakers' guild that made it kept German names for centuries — which is why your side claims it as kin.

But go easy on the pride. The Order didn't *bring* those spices. They came through the **Hanseatic League**, by sea from Venice and Bruges. The Order mostly **taxed** the trade — the pound-toll that helped push this city into revolt.

What wasn't here in 1454: **sugar icing**, and the heart-shaped *katarzynki* (a 19th-century invention). The paprika and cocoa in your jars? New World — a century too early, traps for sharp noses.

A recipe can belong to everyone and no one. Your side guarded it like a secret too.
