## The cook at the heart of this errand never existed — and Toruń remembers him anyway.

Jordan the cook is a legend, not a record. The tale — a castle cook seized by the townsfolk and blown up together with the tower — was written down in the **1800s**, not 1454. The earliest real trace of any town cook is a single building account from **1520**: a *Stadtkoch* at the Chełmno Gate. Sixty-six years too late to be him.

And "Piccolo"? Pure anachronism — an Italian name no 15th-century tavern in Toruń could wear. In 1454 it's simply a kitchen, a ladle, and a man worth finding.

Why build an errand around someone who may never have lived? Because the sharpest legends are the ones a city refuses to let go of. Keep his name.
