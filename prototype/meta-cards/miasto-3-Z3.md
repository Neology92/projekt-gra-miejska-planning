## The Order really did keep spies in Toruń — and we know their names.

The list you stole is an invention. No single roster of the Secret Council ever existed; keeping one would have been suicide. But the dread behind it was real.

The Order ran genuine informers inside the city, and their names survive in its own archives in Berlin: **Peter Sleszer, Jacob Scolim, Hans Bischoff, Daniel von Ellesch, Jorge von Pitterkaw.** Letters signed only *"N.N."* fed Malbork the dates of the Council's secret meetings — weeks before the revolt.

Commander **Albrecht Kalb** knew a plot was brewing as early as November 1453. He even begged Malbork for leave to negotiate. He was refused — and then too weak to act. He knew everything, and lost everything.

You played a spy tonight. So did real men, for real coin.
