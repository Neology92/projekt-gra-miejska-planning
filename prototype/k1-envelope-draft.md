## TEXT — K1 (player reads this)

---

**Toruń, 3rd of February, the Year of Our Lord 1454**

**Messengers,**

The sealed letter enclosed with this note is not yours to read. Carry it. Protect it. Deliver it before the eighth bell strikes tonight.

You were paid to do a simple thing. The city has made it complicated. Your original contact is not where he was meant to be. You will need to find another way to the recipient.

**Know this:** whoever reads that letter, loses it, or delivers it to the wrong hands will answer for it. Not with money. **With their hands.** You have been warned once. You will not be warned again.

---

**Your path begins here.**

Enclosed: a **map** and a **cipher sheet**.

The map shows marks. Each mark tells you *where to go* — not what you will find. That you must see for yourself.

**Begin at △.** Find it on the map and go there.

At each mark:
1. Look around for a **detail** that matches one of the pictures on the cipher sheet.
2. That picture will point you to the **next mark**. Go there. Repeat.
3. Record each place you identify in the **legend** on your map.

Your chain has **exactly four steps**. It ends where our man is waiting.

Beware false trails. If you find yourself going in circles — or returning to a mark you have already visited — you have strayed. Go back to the last mark you are certain of.

When you reach the end, tell our man the **four places you visited, in order**. That is all he needs from you.

After that, you will receive your next instructions.

---

*Tell no one.*

---

## PLANNING NOTES (nie dla gracza)

- **Groźba**: "With their hands" — fizyczna, medievalna, bez ozdobników. Celowo krótka.
- **Plot twist K2** (pośrednika nie ma) jest *zasiany* w K1 ("Your original contact is not where he was meant to be") — ale nie wyjaśniony. Grupa to odczuje dopiero przy MG po Z1.
- **"Eighth bell"** = godz. 20:00, ósme bicie zegara ratuszowego. Nie nazywamy Tuba Dei (anachronizm).
- **Rekwizyt w kopercie:** list zapieczętowany (noszą całą grę), mapa (9 glifów, bez nazw), karta deszyfrownika (9 piktogramów → następny glif).
- **Wersja TR / wersja Krzyżacy**: K1 jest WSPÓLNA dla obu torów (nie zdradza frakcji). Podział następuje dopiero w K2.
- Ton: rzeczowy, lekko zagrażający — ani epicki, ani przygodowy. Zleceniodawca nie jest wrogiem, ale nie jest przyjacielem.
