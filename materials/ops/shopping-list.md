**Paper to buy for printing:**
- Parchment-texture A4 paper (ivory/cream) — for envelopes, Z3 pergamin, sealed letters.

  - Live ×2: ~40–50 sheets

- Plain A4 white — for MG sheets, actor scripts, decoder cards: standard stock.
- A3 colour (optional) — if Z1 map needs larger format for legibility.
